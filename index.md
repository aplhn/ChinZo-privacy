# ChinZo Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** January 1, 2026

ChinZo (“the App”) is developed and maintained by TekDev4. Your privacy is important to us, and this Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the ChinZo app.

---

## Information Collection

ChinZo **does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data**.

* No account registration is required
* No names, email addresses, or contact information are collected
* No location data is collected
* No analytics or tracking tools are used

All content and functionality are available without providing personal information.

---

## In-App Purchases

ChinZo offers **optional voluntary donations** through Apple’s In-App Purchase system.

* Payments are processed securely by Apple
* TekDev4 does **not** receive or store any payment or billing information
* Donations do not unlock, restrict, or alter any app features

---

## Data Storage

All app data, if any, is stored **locally on your device** and is never transmitted to external servers.

---

## Third-Party Services

ChinZo does **not** use third-party analytics, advertising SDKs, or data-collection services.

---

## Children’s Privacy

ChinZo does not knowingly collect any personal information from children under the age of 13. Since no personal data is collected, the app is safe for general audiences.
